Do not silently call optimizer-reset warm starts exact resumption.

Frontend recording captures the actual rendered mission canvas with status/time overlay and saves an actual MP4 under outputs/recordings. Use runtime MIME detection and a backend FFmpeg conversion when native MP4 is unavailable; do not rename WebM to.mp4. Automated post-training capture loads the latest last.pt by SHA, independently runs each task, records the resulting real frontend replay through the same recording functionality, and validates the MP4 by decoding it. Failed latest-weight rollouts retain their true status; they must not be replaced with older weights or expert controls.
